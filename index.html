About Us

International Combat Sports & Martial Arts Academy

International Combat Sports & Martial Arts Academy is Silchar's dedicated training ground for combat sports enthusiasts of all levels. Located inside Lifeline Hospital premises near Das Colony in Ambicapatty, our academy brings world-class martial arts training to the heart of Assam. With a perfect 5-star rating, we pride ourselves on delivering high-quality instruction in a safe, structured, and motivating environment for children, teens, and adults alike.

Our academy operates with focused evening sessions on Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Sunday, ensuring that students can balance their training with school, college, or professional commitments. Whether you are stepping onto the mat for the first time or looking to sharpen your competitive edge, our experienced coaches are committed to helping you achieve your goals — physically, mentally, and spiritually. Join us and become part of a growing community of fighters and champions in Northeast India.

  • 5-Star Rated Academy in Silchar with Proven Excellence
  • Evening Batches Available — Perfect for Students & Working Professionals
  • Expert Coaching in Multiple Combat Sports Disciplines
